# Poker, stage 2 (paragraph for owner approval)

**Title:** How do you play poker?
**Setup:** Poker looks like a game of who gets the best cards.

**Paragraph:**
[1] But you can win a round of poker holding the worst cards at the table. To see how, take the most common kind, called Texas Hold'em. What do you start with? [2] Everyone gets two cards face down that only they can see. Then five more cards are turned face up in the middle, and they belong to everyone: first three, then one, then the last one. Your hand is the best five cards you can make from your two and those five. So what is everyone betting on? [3] Before the shared cards come out, and after each step, everyone bets that their hand will win. All the chips go into one pile in the middle, the pot. On your turn you can put in the same as the last bet, put in more, or fold, which means throwing your cards away and giving up the pot. So how does a round end? [4] If everyone else folds, the last player left takes the pot and never has to show their cards. They might have had the worst cards at the table. [5] If two or more players are still in after the last bet, they show their cards, and the best hand wins. A pair beats no pair, two pairs beat one, and so on up to the best of all, the royal flush: ten, jack, queen, king and ace, all hearts or all of one other suit. Playing a round a day, you'd wait about 85 years for one.

**Bold line:** The best cards only win if they're still in the game.

**Go deeper:** Betting as if you have great cards when you don't is called bluffing, and it's a normal, allowed part of the game, not cheating.
