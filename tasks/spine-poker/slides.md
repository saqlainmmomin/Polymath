## Slides

### Slide 1
**Headline:** How do you play poker?
**Setup:** Poker looks like a game of who gets the best cards.
**Text:** But you can win a round of poker holding the worst cards at the table. To see how, take the most common kind, called Texas Hold'em.
**Hook:** What do you start with?
**Picture:** A card table from above with three players' cards face down. The chips in the middle slide to one player. Then every hand turns over: an ace and a king, two queens, and the winner's 2 and 7 of different suits, the worst cards at the table.
**Picture labels:** THE WORST CARDS

### Slide 2
**Headline:** You start with two cards only you can see
**Text:** Then five more cards are turned face up in the middle, and they belong to everyone: first three, then one, then the last one. Your hand is the best five cards you can make from your two and those five.
**Hook:** So how does the betting work?
**Picture:** Your two cards lift at one corner so you can peek. Five cards turn face up in the middle: three, then one, then one. Five of the seven light up yellow.
**Picture labels:** YOUR TWO · EVERYONE'S · YOUR HAND

### Slide 3
**Headline:** Every bet goes into one pile in the middle, the pot
**Text:** Players bet before the shared cards come out, and after each step. On your turn you can put in the same as the last bet, put in more, or fold, which means throwing your cards away and giving up the pot.
**Hook:** So who takes the pot?
**Picture:** Chips slide from each player into one pile in the middle. Then the three choices side by side: a matching stack, a taller stack, and two cards thrown away.
**Picture labels:** THE POT · THE SAME · MORE · FOLD

### Slide 4
**Headline:** If everyone else folds, the last player left takes the pot
**Text:** They never have to show their cards. They might have had the worst cards at the table.
**Hook:** But what if two or more players are still in?
**Picture:** Two players throw their cards away, one after the other. The pot slides to the last player, whose two cards stay face down, then lift just enough to show a 2 of clubs and a 7 of diamonds.
**Picture labels:** FOLDS · FOLDS · NEVER SHOWN

### Slide 5
**Headline:** If two or more players are still in, the best hand wins
**Text:** After the last bet, they show their cards. A pair beats no pair, two pairs beat one, and so on up. The best of all is the royal flush: ten, jack, queen, king and ace in one suit, like all hearts. In Texas Hold'em, playing a round a day, you'd wait about 85 years for one.
**Bold:** The best cards only win if they're still in the game.
**Picture:** Two hands turn face up: a pair against two pairs, and the two pairs win. Then a short ladder climbs to the top rung, five hearts from ten to ace in yellow.
**Picture labels:** A PAIR · TWO PAIRS · ROYAL FLUSH

### Go deeper
Betting as if you have great cards when you don't is called bluffing, and it's a normal, allowed part of the game, not cheating.
