# bluetooth v2 spine (style: Wireframe). Built fast from the owner's chat answer; no paragraph gate, no approver.

1. **Title (owner's words):** How does Bluetooth work?
2. **Card 1 headline:** Bluetooth is a short-range radio link that shares its airwaves with Wi-Fi
   Hook: So how do two devices find each other?
3. **Card 2 headline:** First, the two devices introduce themselves and swap keys
   Hook: So once they're connected, how do they cope with all that Wi-Fi?
4. **Card 3 headline:** Then they hop from channel to channel, up to 1,600 times a second
   Hook: But for hopping to work, both devices must land on the same channel every time.
5. **Card 4 headline:** One device sets the order of channels and the other follows it
   Hook: So what does all that radio cost in battery?
6. **Card 5 headline:** Short range and low power let tiny batteries run for hours (Classic streams, Low Energy sends bursts and sleeps)
   Bold: Bluetooth is a short-range radio link: two devices introduce themselves once, then hop between channels together to stay clear of Wi-Fi.
7. **More:** profiles (agreed rule sets for one job), one plain sentence.
