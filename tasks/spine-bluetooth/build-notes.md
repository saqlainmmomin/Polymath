# Bluetooth, build notes (steps 2 to 5 of /topic; no phone step, nothing committed)

## What was made
- **Style: wireframe** (black, fine white wire, one pink). Why: the topic is signals and a radio dial, which suits wire objects and one pink for "Bluetooth" (the weak link, the one channel, the hop, the piece). Pink only ever means Bluetooth. Drafting would have made the dial a flat box; Airbrush has no dial or router parts either, so all three needed new parts.
- `ios/Polymath/Topics/bluetooth.json` (4 cards plus `more`, Go deeper in the `more` note), `tasks/spine-bluetooth/slides.md` (the spine), `tools/voice/bluetooth.txt` (script), `ios/Polymath/Resources/voice-bluetooth.*` (rendered, 60.9 s), `ios/Polymath/Scenes/bluetooth.json`.
- **New parts added to the Wireframe kit** (`ios/Polymath/WireframeParts.swift`, listed in its `names`, sizes in `PART_BOX` in `tools/scene/scene.py`): `dial` (a flat radio dial with a back slab, `n` ticks, a hatched Wi-Fi block, one pink channel with a pointer, a hit cross and a riding piece; params `ticks wifi chanOn chan hit piece`), `router` (a Wi-Fi box with antennas and lights), `earbud`. The Xcode project was regenerated by the voice render step.

## Fact recheck (fresh agent, against facts.md)
No hard errors; every number printed is on the sheet. It flagged overclaims that are in the **approved paragraph's own wording**, so I left them and list them here:
1. "If it lands on Wi-Fi, only that piece is lost": the sheet says "busy" channel; word choices forbid "busy Wi-Fi". A landing under Wi-Fi is not always a loss (card 3's hops go through the block with no cross-out; card 4 shows one landing as a loss).
2. "Bluetooth can't win by being stronger, so it never stays in one place": "so" is a cause the sheet does not state; Class 1 Bluetooth can be strong.
3. "weak signal" / "Wi-Fi fills a room with stronger ones" drops the "can" of the setup.
4. "over a hundred in one blink" only holds at the maximum rate; the text has "up to 1,600" right before it.
5. "a split second" is a duration the sheet does not state ("a short skip").
Fixed from the same check (not approved wording): the card 2 notes and label now say "One Wi-Fi network: about a quarter"; the card 2 picture no longer crosses out the channel when it slides into the Wi-Fi block (the hook says "could land", and the loss is card 4's story) and its label reads "BLUETOOTH COULD LAND ON WI-FI".
