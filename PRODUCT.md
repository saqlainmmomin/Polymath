# Tell Me How

## The goal
**Make people curious.** Tell Me How exists to spark curiosity, so people want to read more, know more and learn more. It should happen organically: the reader is fascinated and keeps swiping because they want to, not because they are being taught.

## What that means for every topic
- **Effortless.** Reading a card should take no effort. If a card needs concentration, it has failed.
- **Fascinating first.** Open with the surprise, the odd fact, or the question the reader didn't know they had. The explanation is the payoff for curiosity the card already created.
- **Something for everyone.** Not just cars and machines. Cover many kinds of topics so almost everyone finds at least one that relates to their own life: their body, food, home, phone, money, nature, cities, sport, music, animals, everyday mysteries.
- **Ends with a pull.** Every topic leaves a thread worth following ("Go deeper"), so one answer leads to the next question.

## What it is not
Not edtech: no lessons, quizzes, scores or homework tone. It sits between entertainment and education, closer to entertainment.

## Format (current)
A shorts feed on a phone. Swipe up for a new random topic; swipe sideways through its cards. Each topic is one big question (its title) answered in 1–6 cards, each a headline, an animated picture and a short paragraph. Pictures that can be touched say so with a small fading cue. How to write one: `RECIPE.md`. How it is built: `core/BLOCKS.md`. Plan and decisions: `tasks/todo.md`.
